    How TTK is figured

    Time from first bullet to the kill, target at 100 HP, holding the trigger (no reload, no travel time). Chest = full body damage · Leg = the game's own limb-hit time from the source · Armored = damage through a plate (below). Fewer shots + faster fire rate = lower TTK.

    Ammo vs armor: the delta rule

    What gets through a plate is decided by your ammo grade minus their armor level — not by raw damage. This is straight from the game's combat simulator:
    Δ = ammo − armorDamage throughWhat it feels like
    +2 or more100%plate does nothing
    +175%"why's he still up"
    0 (even)50%fair trade
    −1 or less0%immune till the plate breaks
    One level under their ammo halves your protection; one level over makes you immune. Switch to Armored above and set both to see it per gun.

    Why "armored" is the worst case

    The armored TTK assumes the plate is at full durability the whole time. In a real fight your shots chip the plate down (that's armor damage), so the true kill lands somewhere between the armored number and the bare one — which is why each gun shows its bare TTK too. When you're immune (Δ ≤ −1), nothing kills through the intact plate: strip it first — the list re-ranks by how fast each gun breaks armor. Shotguns read per-pellet at the source, so their model TTK is overstated up close.
